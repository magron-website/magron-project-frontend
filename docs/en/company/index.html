About Us

Founded in 2004, Magron specializes in manufacturing and supplying ferrofluid and related components.

They are mainly used to shield active & inactive gases and dust, and for sensors.

Our products are used in semiconductors, solar cells, display equipment, electronics, automobiles, and more.

They are applied across diverse fields including CVD, PVD, sputtering, ion implanters, thin film, robots, single-crystal growth manufacturing equipment, aerospace, nuclear power, and nuclear fusion.

Our fast turnaround, high quality, and competitive prices will satisfy you.
