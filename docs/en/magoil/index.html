Waterproofing Principle

The MAGSEALED structure keeps ferrofluid between the reel's Rotor Collar (Magnetic body) and the Magnet, reducing water and debris ingress. The ferrofluid held within the magnet's field forms a liquid barrier (Seal Area) around the rotating part.

Even if seawater and salt enter from outside, the ferrofluid layer formed around the Magnet acts as a MAG SHIELD, reducing penetration to internal parts and helping prevent corrosion and contamination of gears, bearings, and the rotating shaft.

How ferrofluid blocks water

1As the magnets are brought closer bit by bit, the ferrofluid attracts and draws together.

2Bringing the magnets even closer unifies the ferrofluid, blocking top and bottom.

3The ferrofluid film blocks the water.

Reel Internal Waterproofing Diagram

Inside the reel are several rotating parts such as the Rotor, Body, and CRBB (Corrosion Resistant Ball Bearing). If seawater or salt enters between these parts, corrosion, noise, increased rotational resistance, and reduced durability can occur.

Without ferrofluid (waterproof ✗), seawater penetrates into the rotor; with ferrofluid applied (waterproof ✓), the ferrofluid film blocks the penetration path and protects the internal parts.
